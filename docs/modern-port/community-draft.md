## Suggested Listing Copy

### Stand and Hold

Humanity builds a response instead of simply waiting for the world to fall.

Stand and Hold is an original human-resistance mod foundation for hostile-world and infection-style scenarios. Recover tissue samples, establish a command post and lab, fund research, and watch a small group of defenders hold a checkpoint. Shared world progress, supplies and saved objectives connect the loop rather than treating soldiers as unrelated spawn eggs.

This NeoForge rewrite is an early technical test candidate, not the preserved Forge 1.12 alpha or a complete military overhaul. The demonstration uses configurable vanilla zombies. External threats are optional, but **no working modern infection-mod combination is advertised**: the exact tested Contagion release fails with the pinned loader. Do not claim SRP compatibility from the legacy branch.

### Demonstrable Foundation

- Persistent shared human progression, data-driven research/missions and local/global supplies.
- Command Post, Research Lab and Supply Crate, server-authoritative menus and bounded loaded-time work.
- Two original human silhouettes, bounded deployment/defence and small checkpoint/Main Base foundations.
- Basic equipment/prototype projectile, regional threat records and capped raids/reinforcements.
- Read-only guide/help commands and an operator-assisted [first playable test loop](showcase-loop.md).

### Quality Work Before Broad Testing

Independent new-user observation, packaged installation, real multiplayer/load testing and a compatible optional infection integration require their own evidence. Current art, equipment and structures are alpha-scale, not final detailed military content. The short loop uses Creative/debug infrastructure; do not describe a complete Survival economy.

### Longer-Term Design Topics

More distinctive unit roles and animation, richer base/logistics behavior and broader threat mappings can build on the foundation. Vehicles, aircraft and further loaders remain separate design/dependency/performance decisions, not available features or dated promises.
